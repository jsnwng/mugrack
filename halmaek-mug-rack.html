Design notes

Handles point diagonally into the empty corners, which lets the 2 × 2 grid sit close together.

Posts are at the edge midpoints, clear of the mugs, with pegs that seat in sockets on the base of the next module up.

In the cooler, the notched cardboard wall separates the dry ice bay from the stacks. A second block on a shelf gives the upper tier its own cold. The layout is a full 2 × 2 stack plus a half module of 2, both two tiers high: 12 mugs in a 15 in run. The half module's handles point diagonally like the full module's, so it adds almost no width. Keep the last plate clear of the drain block near the hump.

Measured: mug 3.25 in rim and base, 5.5 in tall, handle 1.75 in out and 4 in tall. Midea well 17.7 × 13.4 × 27.9 in from the spec photo, with a compressor hump 8 in high and 7.25 in deep across the full width. Igloo flat floor 15 × 12 in (flat only, before the wheel-well hump). Still estimates: the cooler's hump height, the Midea's wall taper, and the dry ice block size.
